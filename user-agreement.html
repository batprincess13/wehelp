1. Platform Role

WeHelp is a coordination platform. WeHelp helps connect clients with available independent workers who may provide event babysitting, home babysitting, cleaning support, or other services that may be added later.

WeHelp is not a domestic worker agency, employment agency, manpower agency, school, daycare, medical provider, or emergency service provider. Workers are independent service providers and are not employees of WeHelp unless expressly stated in a separate written agreement.

WeHelp may help coordinate booking details, share booking information with an available worker, collect or facilitate payment when arranged, and set basic platform rules for safety, service scope, and respectful working conditions.

2. Services Covered

Current services may include:

  • Event Babysitting: child supervision and assistance during weddings, birthdays, receptions, hotel events, church events, company or family events, and similar occasions.
  • Home Babysitting: short-term child supervision and assistance at the client’s home or agreed location.
  • Cleaning Support: regular cleaning, general cleaning, or related cleaning support depending on the worker’s availability and the agreed scope.

Service availability depends on worker availability, location, schedule, service fit, client requirements, and final confirmation.

3. Booking Requests and Confirmation

  • Submitting a request does not automatically confirm a booking.
  • Bookings must be requested at least two days before the preferred schedule.
  • Same-day and next-day bookings are not available unless WeHelp later confirms otherwise.
  • WeHelp or the assigned worker may request additional information before accepting a booking.
  • Final service details, schedule, worker availability, transportation, applicable fees, and payment arrangement must be confirmed before the booking is finalized.

WeHelp may decline, cancel, or reschedule a request when the service is unavailable, outside the accepted scope, unsafe, unclear, or not suitable for the available worker.

4. Payment Terms

  • A ₱100 booking confirmation payment is required before a booking is finalized.
  • This ₱100 confirmation payment is deducted from the full service amount and is not an additional fee.
  • The remaining balance is settled once the service is done, unless a different arrangement is confirmed before the booking.
  • The ₱100 confirmation payment may be given in cash directly to the assigned worker or sent through WeHelp online payment channels when arranged.
  • The final balance may be paid directly to the assigned worker or through WeHelp if that option is arranged.
  • Transportation, off-site fees, out-of-town fees, venue fees, parking, entrance fees, meal allowance, and similar costs are shouldered by the client when applicable.

Published prices are starting rates and may change depending on location, schedule, service type, service duration, venue setup, travel difficulty, worker availability, and final agreed scope.

5. Client Responsibilities

The client agrees to:

  • Provide complete and truthful booking information.
  • Provide the correct service location, venue name, schedule, and contact details.
  • Keep a parent, guardian, or authorized adult reachable during babysitting bookings.
  • Provide a safe, respectful, and reasonable working environment for the assigned worker.
  • Provide access to drinking water, restroom use, and a reasonable waiting or seating area.
  • Provide meal or meal allowance if the booking exceeds four hours or crosses a meal period. Meal allowance starts at ₱150 and may vary depending on location.
  • Shoulder transportation and applicable venue-related costs when required.
  • Give clear instructions and avoid assigning duties outside the agreed service scope.

6. Babysitting Terms and Limits

Event Babysitting

  • Published event babysitting rates apply to one child up to eight years old unless WeHelp confirms otherwise.
  • Event babysitting may include supervision, meal or snack assistance, bathroom breaks, quiet play, comfort, rest support, and moving with the child within agreed event areas.
  • Booking time may count from the worker’s arrival at the agreed meeting location until release by the client, parent, guardian, or authorized adult.
  • Off-site, hotel, reception, formal venue, out-of-town, late-ending, or multi-location events may require additional fees or special arrangements.

Home Babysitting

  • Home babysitting may include supervision, age-appropriate play, reading, drawing, quiet activities, assistance with parent-prepared meals or snacks, nap or rest support, bathroom reminders, basic hygiene assistance, and tidying child-used items during the booking.
  • Bathing assistance may be included only if requested by the parent or guardian and accepted by the assigned worker.
  • Mild home-care support may be accepted only for very light sickness such as slight fever, cough, or colds, subject to worker acceptance.

Not Accepted

  • No overnight sitting.
  • No special care, emergency care, or possible emergency cases.
  • No medical procedures, medication administration, or clinical care unless separately confirmed by a qualified person and accepted in writing.
  • No child transportation alone unless specifically agreed in advance.
  • No tutoring, cooking, heavy cleaning, or unrelated household work unless separately agreed.

7. Cleaning Support Terms

  • Cleaning requests are reviewed based on the type of space, estimated size, current condition, location, schedule, and worker availability.
  • Regular cleaning is intended for basic upkeep such as dusting, sweeping, mopping, and surface wiping.
  • General cleaning, mold treatment, hard-water stain removal, deep grout scrubbing, glass descaling, post-construction cleaning, and similar heavier work may require separate pricing or may be declined if outside the worker’s available scope.
  • For cleaning jobs expected to take more than four hours, at least one 15-minute worker break is included in the service arrangement.

8. Worker Safety and Conduct

WeHelp may decline, cancel, or end a booking if the assigned worker is placed in unsafe, inappropriate, disrespectful, abusive, or unreasonable conditions, or if the requested duties go beyond the agreed service scope.

Clients may be asked to provide optional identification for worker safety, especially for off-site, event, out-of-town, or higher-risk bookings. If a worker requires reasonable identification before accepting a booking, the client may choose whether to proceed.

9. Personal Information and Privacy

WeHelp may collect booking-related information such as the client’s name, contact number, preferred communication method, service needed, preferred date and time, service location, venue address, and optional identification when needed for worker safety.

WeHelp uses this information to review requests, coordinate with available workers, confirm booking details, communicate with the client, process or verify payment when arranged, address safety concerns, and maintain booking records.

By sending a booking request, the client agrees that WeHelp may share relevant booking details with the assigned or potential independent worker for coordination purposes.

WeHelp will not intentionally sell client personal information. WeHelp may disclose information when necessary to complete the booking, comply with legal obligations, address safety issues, prevent abuse or fraud, or respond to lawful requests.

The client is responsible for ensuring that any information provided about a child, family member, guest, venue, or household is accurate and shared with appropriate authority or consent.

10. Limitation of Liability

WeHelp’s role is limited to coordination unless expressly agreed otherwise in writing. Services are provided by independent workers. To the fullest extent allowed by law, WeHelp is not liable for acts, omissions, negligence, misconduct, loss, injury, damage, delay, cancellation, or dispute arising from the independent worker’s actual service performance, the client’s instructions, the condition of the service location, or circumstances outside WeHelp’s reasonable control.

Nothing in this Agreement is intended to exclude liability that cannot legally be excluded under applicable law.

Clients are expected to supervise overall arrangements, provide accurate instructions, keep reachable during babysitting services, and maintain a safe environment.

11. Changes to These Terms

WeHelp may update this User Agreement from time to time as services, policies, pricing, payment channels, or platform operations change. The latest version posted on this page will apply to future booking requests.
