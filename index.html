Out-of-town bookings

Out-of-town and farther-location event requests are welcome and reviewed based on location, travel difficulty, schedule, worker availability, and return transportation needs.

  • Round-trip transportation must be covered by the client
  • Safe return transport must be arranged for late-ending events
  • Farther locations, difficult travel, or late schedules may be quoted separately

Worker Safety and Booking Conditions

WeHelp is a coordination platform, but all bookings must meet basic safety, respect, and working-condition standards.

Client responsibilities

  • Provide a safe and respectful environment for the assigned independent worker
  • Give clear instructions for the child’s care and event movement
  • Keep a parent, guardian, or authorized adult reachable throughout the booking
  • Provide access to drinking water, restroom use, and a reasonable waiting or seating area
  • Provide meal or meal allowance if the booking exceeds 4 hours or crosses a meal period. Meal allowance starts at ₱150 and may vary depending on location.

Platform protection rule

WeHelp may decline, cancel, or end a booking if the assigned worker is placed in unsafe, inappropriate, disrespectful, or unreasonable conditions, or if the requested duties go beyond the agreed service scope.

  • Transportation fee is shouldered by the client
  • Venue fees, entry fees, and parking fees are shouldered by the client when applicable
  • Off-site/event requests may require optional client ID verification for worker safety

Regular or General Cleaning?

Use this guide if you are not sure what type of cleaning to request.

Choose Regular Cleaning if

  • The space is maintained regularly
  • You need dusting, sweeping, mopping, and surface wiping
  • There is no heavy buildup, mold, or hard-water stain problem
  • You need basic upkeep cleaning

Choose General Cleaning if

  • The space has not been cleaned recently
  • There is dust buildup in corners or edges
  • You need more detailed attention than regular upkeep
  • There are light stains or areas that need extra work

General cleaning, mold treatment, hard-water stain removal, deep grout scrubbing, glass descaling, and post-construction cleaning are quoted separately.

Booking and Payment Rules

These rules apply to event babysitting, home babysitting, and cleaning requests.

Booking

  • Bookings must be requested at least 2 days before the preferred schedule
  • Same-day and next-day bookings are not available at this time
  • Requests are subject to worker availability, location, service fit, and final confirmation
  • Client ID is optional but may be requested for off-site/event services for worker safety

Payment

  • Clients may pay the assigned worker directly or pay through WeHelp if that option is arranged
  • Payment must be confirmed before service starts
  • If payment is made through WeHelp, WeHelp acts as a payment collection facilitator for the confirmed booking
  • Transportation, off-site fees, out-of-town fees, venue fees, parking, meal allowance, and similar costs are shouldered by the client when applicable
WeHelp is a coordination platform, not an employer or service agency. Services are independently provided by the assigned worker and are subject to availability, location, agreed scope, off-site or out-of-town requirements, and final confirmation.
