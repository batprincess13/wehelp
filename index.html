Common questions

Are cleaning materials included?

Yes. Basic cleaning materials are included. Specialty chemicals, stain treatments, mold treatment, and heavy-duty equipment are quoted separately.

Is the ₱850 rate for deep cleaning?

No. The ₱850 rate is for regular room cleaning up to 12 sqm. Deep cleaning or heavy buildup requires a separate quote.

Is the bathroom included in the room rate?

No. A small toilet and bath up to 2 sqm can be added for ₱500. Larger or heavily stained bathrooms are quoted separately.

Do you serve outside Marikina?

This starter page is written for Marikina. Nearby areas may be accepted depending on schedule and travel requirements.
